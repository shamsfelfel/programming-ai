A mixed test on Lesson 10 alone: what * 2 means on each of the two containers, why one array has one type, what happens when you write a decimal into an integer array, and how to read a shape. Every question is labelled with its difficulty and the topics it covers, and each topic links back to the paragraph that teaches it.
